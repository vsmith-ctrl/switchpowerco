This site collects
nothing about you.

That is not a promise about how carefully we handle your data. It is a statement about the site's construction. There is nothing here to collect with.

What this site does not do

switchpowerco.com is a set of static pages. It has no database, no server-side code and no accounts. Specifically:

  • No forms

    There is no form anywhere on this site. No name field, no email field, no address, no bill upload, no quote request. Nothing you can type into and submit.

  • No cookies

    The site sets no cookies of any kind, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss. It also uses no local storage, no session storage and no browser fingerprinting.

  • No analytics or tracking

    No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no advertising tags, no session recording, no heatmaps, no A/B testing tools. We do not know who visits this site or what they read.

  • No third-party requests

    Every file this site loads comes from this domain, including the typeface. Your browser contacts no other company while you read these pages. There are no embedded videos, maps, chat widgets or social buttons.

The two things that are true

We would rather be precise than sound cleaner than we are.

Our host keeps server logs
This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure records standard request data such as IP addresses and browser user agents, for security and operational purposes. Those logs belong to GitHub and we have no access to them. GitHub's privacy statement governs that data.
If you email us, we have your email
The addresses on this site are ordinary mailto links that open your own mail program. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to write and send a message. If you do, we hold your email address and whatever you wrote, in our mailbox, and use it only for what you wrote to us about. The next section says exactly what that covers.

What you might send us, and what we do with it

The only personal information we ever hold is what you choose to email us. Depending on why you write, that is:

  • A question

    Your name and email address, and whatever you wrote. We use it to reply to you.

  • Your electricity bill

    If you ask us for real numbers, you will send a bill and your address. A bill carries your name, service address, utility account number and usage history. We use it to work out what solar and storage would do at your house, and to do that we may share it with Continuum, who designs the system. We share it for that purpose and no other.

  • Asking to join the team

    Your name, contact details, and anything you tell us about your experience, such as a CV. We use it to talk to you about the role and to decide whether to offer you one.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, in the sense California law gives those terms. We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked a question. We do not pass your details to a data broker or a lead aggregator, and we do not disclose them to anyone for their own direct marketing. Beyond Continuum in the case above, the only other company that holds your email is the provider that runs our mailbox, which stores it for us and may not use it for anything else.

We keep an email for as long as we are talking to you, and delete it within twelve months of the conversation ending unless you become a customer, or the law requires us to keep it. Job enquiries are kept for as long as California employment record-keeping rules require, then deleted.

If you go on to become a customer, Continuum will give you their own paperwork and privacy terms, and you can decide what you want to share with them from then on.

If you have emailed us and you want to know what we hold, have it corrected, or have it deleted, ask, and we will. Write to info@switchpowerco.com. We may need to check that the request comes from the same address that wrote to us.

If you are in California

California residents have rights over personal information a business holds about them, including the right to know what is held, to have it deleted or corrected, to limit the use of sensitive information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

Because this website collects nothing, there is usually nothing for those rights to apply to. The exception is an email you have chosen to send us. For that, the request address above reaches a person and we will act on it. You can also have someone you authorise make the request for you. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control
Browsers can send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. This site does not track you across other sites, or at all, and does not let any third party do so, so there is nothing for those signals to switch off. We treat them as an opt-out of sale and sharing anyway, and since we do neither, every visitor already has that.
Children
This site is meant for homeowners and for adults looking for work. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly hold personal information about children.

This page describes how this website works. It is not legal advice, and it does not describe the separate terms that apply to a solar contract, a credit application or an installation agreement. Those come with their own disclosures.

If this page changes

If the site starts doing anything this page does not describe, this page changes first, in the same update. The date at the bottom shows when it last changed, and we review it at least once a year. If a change affects what we do with something you have already sent us, we will email you about it before it applies.
